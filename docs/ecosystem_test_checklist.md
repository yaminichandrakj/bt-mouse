# v1.6.0 / v1.7.0 hands-on checklist

Nothing in v1.6.0 has run on real hardware yet. Go through this list once with the laptop,
phone and tab. Note anything that fails and hand it to the next session with the step
number.

## Setup
1. Install the v1.6.0 APK on the phone and the tab, and the v1.6.0 EXE on the laptop.
2. On each Android device, open **Devices** (the icon next to the connection pill) and:
   - turn on **Lanyard control** (Accessibility);
   - turn on the **Lanyard keyboard**;
   - tap **Allow background use**.
3. On the laptop, open **Devices**. Both devices should appear: the phone right of the
   monitor and the tab in the strip at the bottom. Drag the tab against the left edge.

## Laptop keyboard and mouse
4. Push the mouse off the right edge. The phone should show a pointer and a blue frame, and
   the laptop should show "Keyboard and mouse → phone" at the top.
5. Click an app icon on the phone (it should open). Drag to scroll a list. Use the wheel to
   scroll. Right-click should be Back.
6. Tap a text field and type. Repeat with the Lanyard keyboard selected, and check that
   arrows, Backspace and Ctrl+A/C/V work.
7. Push back off the left edge of the phone. The cursor should land on the laptop.
8. Press Ctrl+Alt+→ and then Ctrl+Alt+→ again (it should cycle through the devices), then
   Ctrl+Alt+Home.
9. While on the phone, turn off the phone's Wi-Fi. Control should come back to the laptop
   within about 3 seconds.
10. Check that phone → laptop trackpad still works while the laptop's hooks are running.

## Always connected
11. Swipe Lanyard away from recents. It should stay connected (notification still there).
12. Reboot the phone and don't open the app. It should reconnect by itself.
13. Turn Wi-Fi off and on. It should reconnect within a few seconds.

## Clipboard and files
14. Copy text on the laptop and paste it on the phone and the tab.
15. Copy text on the phone, then open Lanyard, or use the Quick Settings tile, or Share ›
    Send with Lanyard. Paste it on the laptop.
16. Send a photo from the phone to the laptop. It should appear in Downloads\Lanyard with
    a tray message.
17. Send a file over 200 MB from the laptop to the phone and turn Wi-Fi off halfway. Send
    it again; it should resume.
18. Use Explorer › Send to › Lanyard (after **Add to Explorer's Send to menu**).
19. Send a file from the phone to the tab (Devices › Your devices › tab › Send files).

## Streams and library
20. On the phone, tap **Share screen** and allow it. On the laptop, **Devices › Live ›
    Watch**. Click and scroll in the window to use the phone.
21. Watch the phone's screen from the tab and tap on the picture.
22. Record something on the phone. Within about a minute it should show up in the laptop's
    Library, and then in the tab's Library.

## Phone → tab control
23. On the phone, **Devices › tab › Control**. Move, tap, scroll with two fingers, use
    Back/Home, and type with the text box.

## v1.7.0 additions
24. Drag an app icon on the phone with the laptop mouse. The icon should move while you
    hold the button, not only when you let go.
25. Take a screenshot on the laptop (Win+Shift+S) and paste it into a chat app on the
    phone.
26. Send a file over 500 MB laptop → phone on the same Wi-Fi. It should be much faster
    than v1.6.0. Allow port 53536 if Windows asks.
27. Turn off the laptop. On the phone, turn on **No PC? Connect a tablet to this phone**.
    On the tab, scan the phone's QR. Then:
    - move the phone's pointer with the tab's trackpad and type with the tab's keyboard;
    - share the phone's screen and turn on the tab's Screen preview;
    - copy text on the tab and paste it on the phone;
    - send a photo from the tab to the phone.
28. Re-scan the PC's QR on the tab and check it goes back to the PC.
