# Lanyard v1.9.2 — Phones over the internet, live typing, smooth PC mirroring

## Live typing is back

- The Keyboard tab has a **Live / Batch** switch next to the text box. **Live** (the default)
  types each letter on the PC as you type it, the way it worked before v1.5. **Batch** waits
  for Send, for when you want to check a sentence first.
- Autocorrect and suggestions work in Live mode: when the phone fixes a word, the PC's copy
  is fixed too. The button at the end presses Enter on the PC.

## Your phones find each other anywhere

- Phones and tablets on different networks (home Wi-Fi and mobile data, say) now reach each
  other over the internet, with the laptop on or off. They show as **Over the internet** in
  Devices, and files, clipboard, Mirror & control and recordings all work.
- It uses the Cloudflare Worker you already set up for internet access. Run `wrangler deploy`
  in `cloudflare-rendezvous` once more to add the relay. The PC tells every paired phone the
  address, and PIN pairing passes it on.
- Everything sent this way is encrypted with your devices' shared key. Cloudflare can't read it.
- Turn it off under Devices › Away from home.

## The laptop calls your phones

- Lanyard for PC now tells phones it has seen on your Wi-Fi that it's running, so they
  connect within seconds instead of waiting for their next retry.
- If the laptop's address changed (another network, a new router), the phone follows it.
- The call is signed with your devices' shared key, so nobody else on the network can send
  your phone to another PC. A phone you disconnected on purpose stays disconnected.

## Smooth video in the laptop's Mirror window

- The Mirror window now plays real video from the phone, like phone-to-phone mirroring
  does, instead of a run of pictures.
- If the video can't start (an older phone app, say) or stalls, the window switches back to
  pictures by itself.

## Every device's recordings, everywhere

- Media › recordings has a new **On your other devices** list: recordings and snapshots made
  on your other phones and tablets, straight from them, no laptop needed.
  **Play** fetches and opens one; the download button keeps a copy on this phone; **Save all**
  copies everything you don't have yet. Saved files show as **Saved on this phone** and join
  your own recordings.
- The laptop's Library now also lists files that are still only on a phone (big ones it
  doesn't copy by itself, or ones on their way), under **On <device>**. **Open** downloads it.
- It works over whatever reaches the other device: the same Wi-Fi, the laptop, or the internet.

## Typing into the phone without switching keyboards (Android 13 and later)

- With Lanyard control on, the laptop now types every key into the phone, arrows, Backspace,
  Enter and Ctrl shortcuts included, while your normal keyboard stays selected.
- Android 9 to 12 still need the Lanyard keyboard chosen for arrows and shortcuts; letters work
  either way.

## Copies on the phone reach the laptop by themselves

- When you copy something in another app, Lanyard notices (with Lanyard control on), opens for a
  split second to read it, and sends it to your devices. Android only lets the app on screen
  read the clipboard, so this is how it gets there.
- Turn it off under Devices › Clipboard › Send copies from other apps. Copies made with no Copy
  button or message are still missed; Share › Lanyard and the Quick Settings tile still work.

## Compatibility and Versioning

- Install over v1.9.1 normally. If you're still on v1.9.0 or older, reinstall once as the
  v1.9.1 notes say.
- The phone-to-phone relay needs the updated Worker (`wrangler deploy`).
- Lanyard for PC bundles PyAV (FFmpeg) for the Mirror window's video.
- Android, Lanyard for PC and the protocol are all version 1.9.2 (Android `versionCode` 36).
