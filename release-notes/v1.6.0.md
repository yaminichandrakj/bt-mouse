# Lanyard v1.6.0 — Your devices, together

Your laptop, phone and tablet now work as one set. Use the laptop's keyboard and mouse on
the phone, copy on one device and paste on another, send files in either direction, watch
a phone's screen on the laptop, and find every recording on every device.

## Use the laptop's keyboard and mouse on your phone or tablet

- **Push the mouse off the screen edge** where the phone sits and it moves onto the phone.
  Push it back the other way to return. You decide where each device sits.
- **Drag-and-drop layout** on the PC: **Lanyard for PC › Devices** shows your monitors to
  scale. Drag a phone or tablet against any edge of any monitor, at any height, and it
  snaps into place. Drag it to the strip at the bottom to use it by hotkey only.
- **Hotkeys**: `Ctrl+Alt+→` / `Ctrl+Alt+←` go to the next or previous device, and
  `Ctrl+Alt+1…9` pick one. **`Ctrl+Alt+Home` always brings the keyboard and mouse back to the
  PC.** The tray menu has **Keyboard and mouse › This PC / your devices** too.
- **You always know where your input goes**: the phone shows a pointer and a blue frame, and
  the PC shows a small "Keyboard and mouse → Phone" note at the top of the screen.
- **Safe**: if the phone drops off the network, control comes back to the PC within about four seconds. The phone's notification has a **Release** button.
- **On the phone**: click to tap, drag to swipe, the wheel scrolls, right-click is Back,
  middle-click is Home, Esc is Back, the Windows key is Home and Alt+Tab is Recents.
- **Typing**: turn on the **Lanyard keyboard** and switch to it to type anything,
  including arrows, Backspace, Enter and Ctrl+C/V/X/A. Without it, letters, numbers and
  punctuation still type into the focused field.
- **One-time setup on each phone or tablet** (Android requires it): **Devices › Let your
  laptop control this device** walks you through turning on **Lanyard control**
  (Accessibility) and the **Lanyard keyboard**.

## Always connected

- **Stay connected** (on by default): the phone keeps itself connected in the background,
  starts again after a restart or an app update, reconnects as soon as Wi-Fi comes back, and
  keeps trying once a minute when the PC is out of reach.
- A quiet notification shows the state, with **Reconnect now**, **Disconnect** and
  **Release** buttons. Android requires this notification for any app that stays running.
- If Android would close Lanyard in the background, **Devices** shows **Allow background
  use**. On Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo and Samsung phones, also set battery use to Unrestricted.

## Clipboard and files

- **Shared clipboard**: copy on the PC and it is on your phone and tablet at once. From a
  phone it is sent while Lanyard is open, while the Lanyard keyboard is active, from
  **Share › Send with Lanyard**, or with the new **Send clipboard** Quick Settings tile.
  Android blocks reading the clipboard in the background, so this cannot be fully
  automatic.
- **Send files anywhere**:
  - **From a phone**: Share › Send with Lanyard, or Devices › Send files.
  - **From the PC**: drop files on the window, use **Devices › Send files…**, or add Lanyard
    to Explorer's right-click **Send to** menu with one click.
- Large files go in pieces, each piece is checked, and a dropped connection **resumes**
  where it stopped. Received files go to **Downloads/Lanyard** on both sides, with a
  notification to open them.
- **Phone ↔ tablet**: files, clipboard, control and streams also go between two Android
  devices, passed along by the PC.

## Live streams between devices

- **Share a phone's screen**: Devices › Live › Share screen. Android asks each time. Any
  other device, or the PC, can watch.
- **Use a phone from its picture**: in the PC's watch window you can click, drag and
  scroll on the phone's screen, plus Back, Home and Recents. A tablet watching a phone
  can tap and swipe on the picture the same way.
- The phone camera, while it streams to the PC as a webcam, is also offered as a stream.
- Pictures are only taken while someone is watching, to save battery and data.

## One library everywhere

- The PC now **collects a copy of every recording and snapshot** made on your phones and
  tablets, filed under Videos, Audios and Snapshots. Every device already browses and
  plays the PC's Library, so everything is available everywhere.

## Control another phone or tablet from a phone

- **Devices › Your devices › Control** opens a trackpad for that device. One finger moves
  and taps, two fingers scroll, and a two-finger tap is Back. It also has Home, Recents,
  Notifications, Hold-to-drag and a box to type text.

## What Android does not allow

- An app cannot control the lock screen, PIN or fingerprint entry, or apps that block
  screen capture (banking, password fields).
- Screen sharing needs a tap to allow it each time it starts.

## Compatibility and Versioning

- Android app version: 1.6.0 (version code 31). Adds the Accessibility service, a
  keyboard (input method), a Quick Settings tile, a share target, start-on-boot and the
  screen-capture permission. Nothing is used until you turn it on.
- Windows companion and server protocol version: 1.6.0. Older phone apps keep working;
  the new features need both sides on 1.6.0.
- Pairing, settings and recordings carry over. The app updates in place.
