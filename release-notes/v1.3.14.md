# BT Mouse v1.3.14

## Phone Camera as PC Camera Source & Virtual Webcam

- **Android Camera2 Wireless Webcam Streamer**:
  - Direct hardware-accelerated camera capture engine using standard Android `Camera2` API and `ImageReader` with `ImageFormat.JPEG`.
  - Supports on-the-fly lens toggling between rear and selfie front cameras with automatic aspect ratio and sensor resolution matching.
  - Granular resolution presets: 360p (low-latency 640x360), 480p (balanced 854x480), and 720p (HD 1280x720) with target frame rates (15 fps, 24 fps, 30 fps).
  - Rate-limited transmission pipeline to preserve wireless throughput across local Wi-Fi UDP and secure Remote WAN WebSocket channels.

- **Android Viewfinder & Controls**:
  - Added dedicated **"Phone as PC Webcam"** card in the **Camera & Mic** utilities tab.
  - Displays real-time local viewfinder preview alongside dynamic telemetry: live FPS, bitrate (kbps), frame resolution, and total frame counter.
  - Built-in runtime permission handling for `android.permission.CAMERA` and dynamic lens flipping while actively streaming.
  - Pulsating `LIVE TO PC` privacy indicator ensuring clear visibility when the camera sensor is actively transmitting.

- **Android Foreground Service Camera Support**:
  - Declared `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_TYPE_CAMERA` in `AndroidManifest.xml` and wired into `CompanionSessionService`.
  - Background session notification displays active webcam status with a direct **"Stop Stream"** action, allowing continuous camera streaming even when switching apps on the phone.

- **PC Companion Virtual Camera & Local MJPEG Server**:
  - Embedded lightweight HTTP daemon running at `http://127.0.0.1:8766/phone_cam/mjpeg`, providing a zero-driver, zero-install MJPEG stream compatible with OBS Studio, VLC, browsers, and video conferencing tools.
  - Snapshot endpoint available at `http://127.0.0.1:8766/phone_cam/snapshot.jpg` and telemetry endpoint at `/phone_cam/status`.
  - Direct `pyvirtualcam` integration allowing the phone's video feed to be routed into OBS Virtual Camera or DirectShow virtual webcams for Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Discord, and Google Meet.
  - Dedicated **"📷 Phone as Webcam"** tab in PC Companion GUI with live viewfinder preview, telemetry readout, quick OBS setup instructions, and one-click snapshot saving.

- **Server Protocol & Testing**:
  - Handlers for `phone_cam_start`, `phone_cam_frame`, and `phone_cam_stop` with thread-safe frame receiver and automatic resource release on client disconnect.
  - Unit tests covering start acknowledgment, frame updates and callback dispatch, and graceful stop state reset.
  - All 31 companion regression unit tests passing.
