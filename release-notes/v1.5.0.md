# BT Mouse v1.5.0

## A new look, a live view of your PC screen, and zoom everywhere

### Phone app
- **New look**: black background, grey for what is selected, white for the main action, and
  colour only for status. Inter typeface, larger text and more room around everything.
- **Five tabs**: Control, Keyboard, Cameras, Media and PC. Tap the connection pill in any
  header to open Connect; Settings is under the gear on the PC tab.
- **See your PC screen** while you work: tap **Screen** on Control or Keyboard. The screen
  sits above the trackpad (or the keys), so you can watch the pointer as you move it.
- **Zoom on every picture**: the PC screen, the phone camera and the PC webcam can all be
  pinched, dragged and double-tapped, in the panel or full screen. Full screen turns the
  phone to landscape.
- **Keyboard on one screen**: a send box, held Ctrl/Alt/Shift/Win, Fn to swap shortcuts for
  F1–F12, arrows and editing keys, and a Numpad switch.
- **Slides** (Media › Slides): the slide in view, big Next and Previous, Blank screen,
  Start/End show, a timer, and volume keys to change slides.
- **PC tab**: CPU, memory, battery and disk; Lock, Sleep, Restart and Shut down; your quick
  apps; and the busiest tasks, with sheets for all tasks and for editing apps.
  Recordings moved to Cameras, where they are made.
- **Connect**: tap Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or Internet to try that link right away, even if the app
  had stopped trying it, or tap **Retry all**.
- The Pen slate has been removed.

### Windows companion
- **Same look as the phone**, with Inter bundled.
- **Sidebar**: Connect, Phone camera, Webcam & apps, Library and Activity, with Settings at
  the bottom and the connected phone shown above it.
- **Screen sharing you can see and stop**: a banner shows while your phone views the
  screen, with **Stop sharing**. Settings has a switch to turn screen viewing off.
- **Zoom in camera views**: scroll or use − / +, drag to move around, double-click to reset
  or go full screen.
- The Drawing slate page has been removed.

## Compatibility and Versioning

- Android app version: 1.5.0 (version code 29).
- Windows companion and server protocol version: 1.5.0.
- Update both. Screen view needs the 1.5.0 companion; older companions ignore the request.
