# BT Mouse & PC Companion v1.3.17 Release Notes

**Release Date:** October 24, 2026  
**Version:** `1.3.17` (`versionCode = 24`)

---

## 1. Native Windows 11 Desktop Companion Architecture (`PyQt6` Fluent Overhaul)

- **Replaced Legacy Tkinter with Hardware-Accelerated `PyQt6` (`pc_server/app_gui.py`):**
  - Re-architected the Windows Desktop Companion from legacy `tkinter` fixed-pixel frames to a true DPI-independent **Qt6 / PyQt6 Fluent Windows 11 desktop application** with native Windows 11 DWM dark titlebar integration (`DWMWA_USE_IMMERSIVE_DARK_MODE`).
  - **Zero Clipped Labels or Hidden Controls:** All sidebars, cards, tables, and action bars use responsive Qt box/grid layouts (`QVBoxLayout`, `QHBoxLayout`, `QScrollArea`, `QStackedWidget`, `QTableWidget`) with logical DPI scaling so text and buttons never clip at 125%, 150%, or 200% Windows display scaling.
  - **8 Dedicated Fluent Pages:**
    1. **Wi-Fi & QR Pairing:** Instant high-contrast QR pairing card + network interface selector + 1-click IP/PIN copy.
    2. **Bluetooth Service:** SPP RFCOMM status, paired device instructions, and Windows Bluetooth settings launcher.
    3. **Remote WAN:** TLS/WSS and LAN WebSocket remote pairing QR generator and port manager.
    4. **Desktop & Startup:** Windows Sign-In auto-start toggle, minimize-to-tray, notifications, and 3-folder quick launchers (`Videos/`, `Audios/`, `Snapshots/`).
    5. **Media Library:** Full searchable, filterable (`All`, `Video`, `Audio`, `Snapshot`) `QTableWidget` with live disk usage gauge and Explorer integration.
    6. **Phone as Webcam:** Large responsive `AspectRatioVideoWidget` viewfinder (`16:9` / `9:16` uncropped fit), live resolution/orientation/FPS/bitrate badges, **Rotate 90°** button, **Save Snapshot**, **Record MP4 to `Videos/`**, **Pop Out / Fullscreen (`⛶ Fullscreen`)**, and OBS / Virtual Camera controls.
    7. **PC Webcam Preview:** Local PC webcam live preview with **Record Video + Mic**, **Save Snapshot**, and **⛶ Fullscreen** viewer.
    8. **Live Monitor:** Real-time packet counter, active client badge, and dark monospace telemetry console.

## 2. Seamless Video & Audio Stream Continuity Across Transport Failover

- **Dynamic Stream Responder Migration (`pc_server/server.py`):**
  - Previously, `_start_camera_stream_for_client` captured the initial transport's `respond` closure inside `_on_frame`, causing frames to keep transmitting on the old path after failover switched transports.
  - Now, `_on_frame` and `_on_stream_end` dynamically look up `self._camera_stream_owner["respond"]`, and `_refresh_camera_stream_lease()` + `LiveAudioManager.touch_owner(..., migrate=True)` automatically migrate the active stream responder whenever `path_switch`, `ping`, `camera_stream_keepalive`, or any authenticated packet arrives on the newly elected transport.
- **Reactive `StateFlow` Stream Multiplexing on Android (`ConnectionCoordinator.kt`):**
  - Converted `latestCameraFrame`, `isCameraStreaming`, `isCameraRecording`, `isAudioRecording`, `isPcLiveAudioActive`, and `isPhoneLiveAudioActive` from static `get()` delegates into reactive `StateFlow` pipelines (`merge` / `combine` / `flatMapLatest`) across Wi-Fi, Bluetooth RFCOMM, and Remote WAN.
  - Eliminated the false 4-second frame starvation timeout in `KeyboardScreen.kt` during rapid failover switches.

## 3. Single Combined Video + Audio Recording (`.mp4` Muxing)

- **Automatic FFmpeg Audio+Video Muxing (`pc_server/server.py`):**
  - When recording the PC webcam with microphone audio enabled (`record_with_audio = true`), stopping the stream now automatically invokes `mux_video_and_audio(video_path, audio_path)` (powered by `imageio-ffmpeg` / `ffmpeg` with `-c:v copy -c:a aac -shortest`) to produce a single synchronized `.mp4` file in `Videos/` and removes the temporary standalone `.wav` track.

---

## 4. Dedicated `Videos/`, `Audios/`, and `Snapshots/` Folders on Both PC and Phone

- **PC Companion (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\BTMouse\`):**
  - Automatically organizes and migrates media into three distinct directories:
    - `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BTMouse\Videos` (PC webcam `.mp4` recordings & Phone camera `.mp4` recordings)
    - `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BTMouse\Audios` (Standalone `.wav` / `.m4a` voice and microphone recordings)
    - `%LOCALAPPDATA%\BTMouse\Snapshots` (`.jpg` still captures from PC webcam and Phone camera)
- **Android App (`Android/data/com.btmouse.app/files/`):**
  - `SnapshotGallery.kt` and `RecordingTransferClient.kt` now store and scan files in dedicated `Videos/`, `Audios/`, and `Snapshots/` folders (with automatic legacy migration from `Pictures/Snapshots` and `Downloads/PC Recordings`), and the **Library** tab includes filter chips for **All**, **Videos**, **Audios**, and **Snapshots**.

---

## 5. Phone-to-PC Livestream Reliability, Clear Orientation Controls & Fullscreen Viewers

- **Fixed "Worked Once and Never Again" Bug (`PhoneCameraStreamer.kt`):**
  - Added `sessionGeneration` tracking to guard against asynchronous `CameraDevice.close()` callbacks nulling out newly opened Camera2 sessions when flipping lenses, changing resolution, or restarting the stream.
  - Added `processAndOrientFrame()` with sensor rotation alignment and adaptive JPEG compression (`<= 35 KB`) so 360p, 480p, and 720p frames never exceed the IPv4 UDP datagram limit.
- **Explicit Orientation & Alignment Controls:**
  - Added **Landscape 16:9 (Horizontal)** and **Portrait 9:16 (Vertical)** mode selectors, a **Rotate 90°** button on both the phone and PC, uncropped `ContentScale.Fit` preview rendering, and live orientation/resolution badges on both devices.
- **Maximize / Fullscreen Viewers on Both Phone and PC:**
  - Added a **⛶ Fullscreen** dialog on the Android app (`FullscreenVideoModal` with pinch-to-zoom up to `4x`, pan, rotate 90°, snapshot, and record controls) for both the **PC Webcam** stream and the **Phone Camera** preview.
  - Added a **⛶ Fullscreen** window (`FullscreenVideoWindow` with `F11` / `Esc` / double-click toggle, rotate 90°, snapshot, and MP4 recording) on the Windows PC Companion for both **Phone as Webcam** and **PC Webcam Preview**.

- **5-Tab Bottom Navigation (`MainScreen.kt`):**
  - Promoted **Cameras** (`KeyboardScreen` Camera & Mic Studio) to a primary first-class tab alongside **Connect**, **Control Pad**, **Media**, and **System**, eliminating the need to hunt through sub-tabs.
