# Lanyard v1.9.7 — Restored Previous Interface

## Interface

- Restored the Android Devices screen and Windows companion pages to their pre-v1.9.6 layouts.
- Removed the redesigned cross-device media gallery and Share Hub screens.

## Clipboard sync

- Kept automatic clipboard sync and the persistent history across PC and phone reconnections.
- Android keeps the recent clipboard list in Devices; tap an entry to copy and sync it again.

## Compatibility and versioning

- Android, Lanyard for PC and the protocol are all version 1.9.7 (Android `versionCode` 41).
