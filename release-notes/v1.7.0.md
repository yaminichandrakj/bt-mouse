# Lanyard v1.7.0 — No PC needed, faster files, live dragging

Builds on v1.6.0 ("Your devices, together"). If you skipped v1.6.0, read its notes first;
everything there is included.

## Phone and tablet without the PC

- **Devices › No PC? Connect a tablet to this phone**: turn it on and the phone shows a
  pairing QR code and PIN.
- **On the tablet**: Connect › Scan PC QR code, and scan the phone's code. The tablet
  connects to the phone over Wi-Fi the way it would to a PC. With no Wi-Fi, turn on the
  phone's hotspot and join it from the tablet.
- **What then works:**
  - The tablet's normal **Control** trackpad and **Keyboard** tabs drive the phone. The
    phone needs Lanyard control turned on.
  - The tablet's **Screen** button shows the phone's screen while the phone shares it
    (Devices › Live › Share screen).
  - Clipboard, file sending, remote control from the Devices screen, and watching the
    phone's screen all work between the two.
- While paired to a phone, the tablet treats that phone as its PC. Scan your PC's code
  again to switch back. The phone itself can stay connected to the PC at the same time.

## Faster files on the same Wi-Fi

- Between a phone and the PC on the same network, files now go over a direct HTTP
  connection (port 53536). This is several times faster than before for large files.
- An interrupted transfer still resumes from what already arrived. If the direct
  connection can't be made (firewall, mobile data, Bluetooth), the transfer quietly falls
  back to the v1.6.0 method.

## Copy pictures, not just text

- Copy a picture on the PC (a screenshot, an image in a browser) and it is on your phone's
  clipboard, ready to paste.
- A picture copied on the phone goes to the PC's clipboard when Lanyard is open or the
  Send clipboard tile is used, like text.

## Live dragging on the phone

- When the laptop's mouse drags on the phone (moving icons, scrolling, sliders), the
  phone now follows the pointer while the button is held. Before, the whole drag played
  back at once when you let go.
- Phones that refuse this fall back to the old way automatically.

## Still to come

- H.264 video for smoother streams, and audio streams through the hub.
- The phone hub doesn't relay between two tablets, and the phone can't watch a tablet's
  screen yet.
- The PC still waits for phones to connect rather than reaching out to them.

## Compatibility and Versioning

- Android app version: 1.7.0 (version code 32). Adds the ZXing library to draw the
  pairing QR code.
- Windows companion and server protocol version: 1.7.0. It opens TCP port 53536 for the
  file fast path; if Windows asks, allow it on private networks.
- Pairing, settings and recordings carry over. The app updates in place.
