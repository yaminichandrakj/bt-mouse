# Lanyard v1.5.1

## BT Mouse is now Lanyard

- **New name**: the phone app is **Lanyard** and the Windows companion is **Lanyard for PC**.
- **New icon**: a cord meeting at a tag, white on black. On Android 13 and later it also
  follows your themed icons.
- Your phone shows up as **Lanyard** in a PC's Bluetooth list when used as a Bluetooth mouse
  and keyboard.

## Fixes and polish

- **Every PC app now shows up** when you browse apps from the phone. Long app lists were
  too big to send in one go and were silently dropped; they now arrive in parts.
- **Real app icons**, loaded for every app in the list and saved on the phone, so quick apps
  show their icons straight away next time, even before the PC answers.
- **Keyboard shortcuts are icons** (copy, paste, cut, undo, redo, select all, switch app,
  desktop, find, save, Task Manager, screenshot). Long-press a key to see what it does.
- **Camera cards**: a clean "Live on PC" / "Off" status pill instead of the joined
  STANDBY badge, and titles that no longer wrap.
- Doubled symbols, all-caps badges and leftover "PC companion" wording are gone from the
  camera, audio, quick-app, Connect and Settings screens.

Nothing else changes: pairing, settings, recordings and in-app updates carry over. The app
keeps its Android package and the Windows file names, so it updates in place.

## Compatibility and Versioning

- Android app version: 1.5.1 (version code 30).
- Windows companion and server protocol version: 1.5.1.
