# Lanyard v1.9.4 — Phone unlock reconnect

## Phone unlock

- When you tap Unlock without an active PC connection, Lanyard now reconnects using the saved pairing before asking for fingerprint verification.
- The phone waits up to 20 seconds for a secure connection and explains how to reconnect if the PC cannot be reached.
- If no PC pairing is saved, the app points you to the QR code on the PC's Connect page.

## Compatibility and versioning

- Android, Lanyard for PC and the protocol are all version 1.9.4 (Android `versionCode` 38).
