# BT Mouse v1.3.8

This release expands the PC companion utilities and improves recording, connectivity, and update handling across Android and Windows.

## What's new

- **Manage PC Quick Apps from Android:** add or remove discovered PC shortcuts, including preloaded entries, from the phone. The PC companion also supports editing the Quick Apps list.
- **Record and manage PC webcam video:** save webcam sessions, optionally include PC microphone audio, and browse recordings from the companion. The phone can list recordings, download them with integrity verification, or play supported files from the PC.
- **Protect recording storage:** set recording age and size limits plus a minimum free-space reserve. Cleanup avoids active recordings.
- **Listen and speak through the PC:** start PC-microphone audio on the phone or use the phone microphone on the PC. Sessions are user-started and can be stopped from the app.
- **Preview a selected screen region:** select a visible browser or player area on the PC and toggle its live preview from Android. The preview stops when turned off or its session ends.
- **Load PC information sooner:** task and performance requests return cached information while fresh process and system data load in the background. Storage, disk activity, and GPU metrics are reported separately; unsupported NPU readings remain unavailable.
- **Recover camera streams:** repeated empty camera reads now end cleanly and report the failure, while Wi-Fi frames are reduced to fit the datagram limit.
- **Improve startup and updates:** configure Windows sign-in startup, improve update replacement/relaunch handling, and hand Android downloads to the system installer.
- **Improve the companion display:** apply DPI-aware rendering and show the running version/path in the window title.

## Fixes and reliability

- Reject Quick Apps updates that contain paths the PC did not discover as installed shortcuts.
- Apply recording retention before starting a new recording, preserve active files, and report when free space prevents recording.
- Return task snapshots without waiting for a slow process scan.
- Keep the app, companion, release tag, and downloadable filenames on version 1.3.8.
